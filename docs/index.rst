Operational Resilience Engineering (ORE)
========================================

**Operations Research + Resilience Engineering =
Operational Resilience Engineering**
------------------------------------------------

Operational Resilience Engineering (ORE) is a developing framework for
modeling vulnerability, resilience, system performance, and intervention
choices in critical operational systems.

ORE connects operations research and resilience engineering to examine how
system states, performance boundaries, constraints, decision spaces, and
interpretive perspectives shape operational outcomes.

Current status
--------------

This is an evolving independent research program. This site will publish
conceptual notes, formal definitions, working artifacts, preprints,
presentations, and reproducible materials as they become available.

Research directions
-------------------

* Formal relations between vulnerability space and resilience space.
* Models of operational state, performance, feasibility, and decision design.
* Robustness, extensibility, rebound, and adaptability as distinct resilience
  capabilities.
* Interpretation and perspective as explicit components of resilience
  decision models.
* Applications to critical infrastructure and other interdependent
  operational systems.
