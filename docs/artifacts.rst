Artifacts
=========

This page indexes citable research artifacts related to Operational Resilience
Engineering (ORE), including journal articles, preprints, technical notes,
software, data, and presentations.

Published articles
------------------

The rebound curve is a poor model of resilience
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Eisenberg, Daniel A., Thomas P. Seager, and David L. Alderson. 2025.
"The Rebound Curve Is a Poor Model of Resilience." *PNAS Nexus* 4 (3):
pgaf052.

This article examines the limitations of the rebound curve as a model of
resilience. It motivates the ORE effort to move beyond observed
performance trajectories toward explicit representations of vulnerability,
resilience strategies, system dynamics, and control processes.

DOI: `10.1093/pnasnexus/pgaf052 <https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgaf052>`_

Why the system performance curve may fall short
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Demmer, Tobias, Daniel Eisenberg, and Riccardo Patriarca. 2026.
"Why the System Performance Curve May Fall Short: A Demonstration Using
Linear Time Invariant Feedback Control." *Environment Systems and Decisions*.

This article uses linear time-invariant feedback control to demonstrate why an
observed system-performance curve can omit the system dynamics, feedback
structure, and control actions needed to explain or improve resilient
performance.

DOI: `10.1007/s10669-026-10142-6 <https://doi.org/10.1007/s10669-026-10142-6>`_

Current manuscripts
-------------------

Quantitative vulnerability analysis
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Eisenberg, Daniel, and David Alderson. 2026. "Towards a Theory of
Quantitative Vulnerability Analysis for Engineering Systems."

This manuscript develops a quantitative theory of vulnerability analysis for
engineering systems. It defines a vulnerability space,
:math:`\mathcal{V} = (S,L,C)`, and treats risk, reliability, adversary, and
safety as distinct perspectives produced through different interpretations of
likelihood and consequence information.

The manuscript is currently being prepared for public preprint hosting and
subsequent submission to *Risk Analysis*. This entry will be updated with a
permanent preprint identifier, DOI, recommended citation, and publication
status when available.
